# Log in to MetaTrack

To access **MetaTrack**, you can sign in using your institutional **FEIDE account** or create an account directly through the registration page if you do not have access to FEIDE.

## Option 1: Sign in with FEIDE

If your institution provides a FEIDE account, you can use it to access MetaTrack.

1. Go to log in page on [MetaTrack](https://metatrack.no/login).
2. Select the **Sign in with FEIDE** option.
3. You will be redirected to the FEIDE authentication page.
4. Select your institution, if requested.
5. Enter your institutional username and password.
6. Complete the authentication process to return to MetaTrack.

Once authentication is successful, you will be redirected to the dashboad page.

![MetaTrack dashboard](/get-started/login/dashboard.png)

## Option 2: Register directly

If you do not have a FEIDE account, you can create a MetaTrack account directly.

1. Open the MetaTrack login page.
2. Select **Register** to access the registration form.
3. Fill in the requested information:
   - **Email:** Your email address.
   - **First Name:** Your first name.
   - **Last Name:** Your last name.
   - **ORCID:** Your ORCID identifier, if available.
   - **Country:** Your country, if applicable.
   - **Institution:** Your affiliated institution, if applicable.
4. Select **Register** to submit the form.

![MetaTrack registration form](/get-started/before-you-start/register-page.png)

After submitting your registration, follow any instructions displayed by the platform to complete the process.

## Having trouble logging in?

If you experience problems with FEIDE authentication or direct registration, contact your institution's IT support or the [MetaTrack support team](/help-and-support).
