# Before you start

Welcome to the Metatrack!

Metatrack provides a platform for organizing microbial research data, managing sample metadata, connecting sequencing information and collaborating with other researchers.

This guide will walk you through the main steps required to start using the platform and manage your research data.

Before creating your first project, we recommend reviewing the information below to make sure you have everything ready.

---

## What can you do with Metatrack?

**Metatrack** helps researchers organize and manage microbial research data throughout their projects.

With **Metatrack**, you can:

- **Create and manage projects:** Organize your research data within dedicated project workspaces.
- **Register samples:** Add microbial samples and describe them using relevant metadata fields.
- **Manage metadata:** Edit existing information and add custom metadata fields when needed.
- **Manage sequencing data:** Associate sequencing experiments and files with registered samples.
- **Collaborate:** Invite other researchers and manage their access permissions.

## Requirements

Before accessing **Metatrack**, make sure you have the following:

### 1. An institutional FEIDE account

You need access to a valid [FEIDE](https://www.feide.no) account to authenticate and log in to **Metatrack**.

![Login Page](/get-started/before-you-start/login-page.png)

If you do not have a FEIDE account, you can also create an account directly through the MetaTrack registration page by selecting the Register option.

![Login Page](/get-started/before-you-start/login-page-highlight.png) ![Register Page](/get-started/before-you-start/register-page.png)

During registration, you will be asked to provide:

Email: Your email address (required).

First Name: Your first name.

Last Name: Your last name.

ORCID: Your ORCID identifier (optional).

Country: Your country (optional).

Institution: Your affiliated institution (optional).

Once you have completed the registration form, click Register to submit your information.

If you do not have a FEIDE account, contact your institution's IT support for assistance.

### 2. Basic project information

Prepare some basic information about your research project:

- Project name.
- Data-owning organization.
- Project description
- Additional project information, if applicable.

### 3. Sample information

If you plan to register samples, we recommend preparing the relevant sample information in advance.

Depending on your research, this may include:

- Sample identifiers.
- Scientific name or organism information.
- Sample collection information.
- Geographic location, when applicable.
- Sample source and isolation information.
- Other relevant metadata.

You can also download the available sample templates in your preferred format:

- **Standard Sample Template:** [CSV](https://api.metatrack.no/templates/samples/sample.csv) | [XLSX](https://api.metatrack.no/api/templates/excel/sample)
- **Extended Sample Template:** [CSV](https://api.metatrack.no/templates/samples/sample_extended.csv) | [XLSX](https://api.metatrack.no/api/templates/excel/sample_extended)
- **Virus Sample Template:** [CSV](https://api.metatrack.no/templates/samples/sample_virus.csv) | [XLSX](https://api.metatrack.no/api/templates/excel/sample_virus)

You do not need to have every possible metadata field available before starting. Additional information can be added or edited later.

### 4. Sequencing information and files (optional)

If you already have sequencing data, you may also prepare:

- Sequencing platform and instrument.
- Library preparation information.
- Sequencing strategy and layout.
- Forward and reverse sequencing files, when applicable.
- MD5 checksums for file integrity verification.

You can download the experiment templates in your preferred format:

- **Paired-End (PE) Experiment Template:** [CSV](https://api.metatrack.no/templates/experiments/experiment_PE.csv) | [XLSX](https://api.metatrack.no/api/templates/excel/experiment_PE)
- **Single-End (SE) Experiment Template:** [CSV](https://api.metatrack.no/templates/experiments/experiment_SE.csv) | [XLSX](https://api.metatrack.no/api/templates/excel/experiment_SE)

Sequencing information can also be added at a later stage.

## Recommended preparation checklist

Before proceeding, use this checklist to make sure you are ready to start.

- [ ] I have access to a FEIDE account.
- [ ] I have the basic information about my research project.
- [ ] I have prepared a list of samples (if applicable).
- [ ] I have reviewed the sample metadata requirements.
- [ ] I have sequencing information and files available (if applicable).

**You are now ready to get started!**
